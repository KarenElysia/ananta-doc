Economy

The more you contribute, the taller the city grows.

Two currencies with clearly separated roles: Chaos Points (earned by chatting) fund the shared tower, while Coins (from gacha and casino) build your own collection.

  • 1–5 points per comment, 60-second cooldown
  • A rare "Chaos Surge!" bonus grants a flat 20 points
  • Coins can be exchanged from points, but never back (keeps the economy healthy)
  • 7 different leaderboards: total & weekly contribution, battle, fishing, and more

Gacha & Cards

Pull, equip, and fuse your way up.

100 coins for a single pull, 900 for ten. Duplicate characters raise their level cap, and items can be equipped (up to 2 slots) to boost your stats.

  • Unique items span 6 grades (D through S+), each with its own individual roll
  • Bag full? Rent a temporary container for +30 slots
  • Fuse 5 items of the same grade into 1 of the next grade up
  • Sell off anything you don't need for coins, in bulk
Fishing

A second daily loop, separate from the market.

Cast a line at Nova Riverside. Fill a 100-entry fishdex across two cycles, or sell your catch on the flea market. Tournaments run automatically.

  • A 0–4 fatigue meter that resets naturally with a short break
  • Filling the fishdex unlocks titles and a sell-price bonus
  • List or buy fish on the flea market, or register caught fish to your dex later
  • A fishing tournament runs automatically every 6 hours, 4 times a day

Exploration

Walk away, and come back to more.

Dispatch your cards on a lazily-evaluated idle expedition. Perfect for players who can't stay glued to the screen — the reward scales with how long you waited.

  • Higher exploration power (gear & level) means a higher success rate
  • Luck stacks the odds for bonus item drops
  • Longer dispatches also raise the quality of bonus drops
  • A weekly server-wide raid pays out once enough expeditions return
Battle & League

Card classes give every matchup a story.

Arena battles apply small bonuses based on class matchups, shown with a lightweight animated duel. The PvP League is a fully automatic, sign-up round-robin tournament.

  • Friendly duels carry no stakes; ranked matches scale rewards with win streaks
  • The PvP League runs automatically every 12 hours, sign-up required
  • Fixed bonuses for the top 3, plus a participation reward for everyone else
  • 6 card classes: Attacker, Defender, Balanced, AoE, Tank, and Boss

Social

The more you connect, the more there is to do.

Four ways to interact with other players — trade, gift, auction, and the flea market — all designed with abuse-resistance built in from day one.

  • Trades escrow items and coins the moment you offer them, not at the last second
  • Gifts are one-way, with daily caps and an account-age requirement
  • Auctions auto-extend on last-minute bids to prevent sniping
  • The flea market lets players buy and sell caught fish directly
Getting Started

You barely need to memorize a thing.

Everything is built around buttons and dropdown menus, so you're never stuck guessing a command. Start with these 11.

/city

Check the tower's progress, donate Chaos Points, and view the contribution leaderboard.

/gacha

Spend coins to pull for characters and items. Odds are shown right in the prompt.

/bag

View your cards and items, then level up, equip, sell, or fuse them.

/fish

Cast a line at Nova Riverside. Your fishdex, status, titles, shop, and market are one tap away.

/explore

Send a dispatch team out on an idle expedition. Pick a duration, then just wait.

/battle

Fight in the Arena. Build your lineup first with `/deck`.

/league

Check the current PvP League and sign up while registration is open.

/trade

Exchange items and coins directly with another player.

/gift

Send coins or items to someone — no confirmation needed on their end.

/casino

Exchange Chaos Points for coins, or try your luck on the slots.

/help

Category-by-category guides for gacha, fishing, battle, exploration, and more.
